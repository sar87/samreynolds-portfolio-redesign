### An interesting time to be in the 'AI space'

With lots of chatter about model alignment (what you ask it to do vs. what a model will do to achieve the results), frontier capabilities, and the dangers of autonomous agents - there are a lot of topics to sink your teeth into. It's hard to know whether to feel excited or existential about AI. There is a broad understanding that this technology is going to fundamentally change how we work, research and live our lives, but how exactly that will manifest is less clear. It is hard to have conversations about AI from a standing start, as you can often end up talking cross purposes. I am trying to use some of my time off to clarify my thinking on AI in Conservation and AI more broadly. There are big differences regarding the implications of a geo-observational model like [TESSERA](https://geotessera.org/), or a small classifier used to distinguish bird calls, and frontier LLMs that are being used to power agentic bot swarms chasing after a goal at any cost. However, they are often tarred with the same broad brush by the term 'AI'. Or, am I wrong to think that these technologies can be separated, should they be considered in the same breath? These are slightly difficult things to untangle.

### Future of the Conservation Evidence Website

Will Morgan and I have been meeting with some database specialists to figure out how we can move the Conservation Evidence (CE) [website](www.conservationevidence.com) into the 21st Century. The CE website has been built slowly and iteratively over many years. The initial structure that was developed is now creaking, and as well as a larger number of autonomous bot accesses to the website, the structure of the data is leading to frequent hitches in website performance. 

Luckily, we have found two people that we are looking to bring on board to help us transition the data structures underlying the CE website - this should not only make the website more stable - but also make the data, and how we serve that data, more flexible. This will improve our ability to be able to integrate CE data into third party tools, such as [IBAT](https://www.ibat-alliance.org/).
