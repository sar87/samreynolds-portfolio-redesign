### Synthesaurus V2 nears completion

This week I have continued to hack away with my friend Claude on Synthesaurus V2. I have enlisted my best friend GSD ([Get Ship Done](http://github.com/open-gsd/gsd-core)), a meta prompting framework that helps when building out larger projects. It helps you to build out a roadmap to a version one of your application, splitting the plan into smaller tasks which can be tackled sequentially (or sometimes in parallel) that involve multiple phases: discussion, research and planning, context creation, execution, gap closing and validation. Each of these phases is carried out in a fresh context window with multiple agents. Some might call it 'tokenmaxxing', but in my experience for larger ideas it helps to get them mostly right the first time. Opposed to relying on Claudes 'inbuilt' planning and execution tools. 

I have got it to the stage where I could run the first very small tests, using our locally hosted qwen model on our big computer 'Sequoia'. The initial results are encouraging! When set up to identify papers on butterfly conservation it performed perfectly, ignoring the redherring papers and the clearly irrelevant papers:

### Conservation Evidence MCP

The minimum viable product is up and running. I have actually found it extremely useful already while working on Synthesaurus - it turns out it is a really simple way of exploring the CE database and asking questions about its contents in a way that bypasses having to code up your own SQL queries. It also contains information about the broader CE project, as well as its people and our other publications. I am planning to show this to the rest of the team at the upcoming team meeting.
