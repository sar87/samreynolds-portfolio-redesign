### Launching the Conservation Evidence Copilot 'Pre-Alpha'

I am not sure if 'pre-alpha' is the correct terminology, but in collaboration with [Mark Elvers](https://github.com/mtelvers) and [Anil](https://anil.recoil.org/) over in computer science, the [Conservation Evidence Copilot](www.conservationevidencecopilot.com) is now live online. Currently, it is invite only, and being tested internally by the Conservation Evidence team. The idea is to try and break it ourselves before we turn it loose for others to play around with. This is the first 'app' I have ever built, so I am expecting some feedback. I already have my own list of features I hope to implement in the near future, which include things like semantic caching, testing some newly released open-source models, and creating a phone optimised version of the app. I look forward to sharing these updates soon.

### Speaking to Civil Servant Fast Streamers

On Wednesday Sadiq and I spoke at a [CSaP](https://www.csap.cam.ac.uk/) event about our research to a couple of groups of civil service fast streamers at various stages along their rotation journey. We talked about our research in AI evidence synthesis, and the inevitable rise of LLMs in government decision making. Our aim was to make it clear that the implementation of AI into decision making processes is not a simple one, not that anyone who thinks about it for more than 30 seconds would think it is. However, there are a lot of government departments who are interested in this idea, and are trying to gear up to use AI to help them draw inferences across all their internal documentation, as well as the academic literature. It was interesting to hear the Fast Streamer's thoughts on this, and to hear their experience of AI use in government. Interestingly, their experiences echo what we have seen elsewhere, where AI is mainly used as a productivity tool for simple tasks like emails and editing reports. I think the big AI revolution that is so often touted as 'happening', and the sentiment that if your organisation is not already AI native, then you are being left behind, is demonstratively false. The majority of people are still tinkering at the edges, and the big productivity gains that are bring promised, are yet to materialise in any meaningful way.
