### Filming for ai@CAM

This week I finished filming for a short video produced by ai@CAM on the Conservation Evidence Copilot I have been developing as part of their ai@CAM sprints funding. Together with Prof Lynn Dicks, we filmed several talking head segments in the Zoology Library and David Attenborough Building on how we hope the Copilot will help conservation decision makers embed evidence into their decision making. I also had the honour of filming endless B-roll of me strolling around the department, walking through meadows and thoughtfully crouching on riverbanks - all while staring thoughtfully into the middle distance. I look forward very much to seeing the first edit next week.
