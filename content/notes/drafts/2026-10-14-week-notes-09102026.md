### Stanford University - Uncommon Dialogue

This week I was invited to attend an event at Stanford University on harnessing AI for Nature. It was a fantastic group of people from a range of tech organisations (i.e., google and anthropic), NGOs (i.e., WWF and TNC), policy (i.e., EPIC) and Academia (i.e., MIT, Stanford, UCSD). Here I have collated some of my reflections from the discussions that we had on the day. I have omitted attributions to any particular statements, as the meeting was undertaken under Chatham House rules. 

The general framing and overarching question for the event was "What can we do together that we cannot do apart? and, what decisions to we need to make to work more together?"

We started with some updates from the perspectives of AI for Nature from three different sectors, Conservation NGOs, Academia and the Technology sector.
